🎯 Our Mission

At PhishGuard AI, our goal is to provide a robust, AI-driven defense mechanism against modern cyber threats. We aim to empower individuals and organizations by instantly identifying malicious links, spear-phishing emails, and dangerous SMS texts (Smishing) before they can cause harm.

⚙️ How It Works

Our web application utilizes advanced JavaScript-based string validation and pattern recognition to simulate an AI security scanner. The system instantly analyzes your input against known threat vectors:

  • URL Analysis: Detects deceptive domains and risky redirects.
  • Content Parsing: Highlights urgency language and financial prompts.
  • Local Storage Sync: Securely maintains your scan history and user preferences completely on the client-side.
